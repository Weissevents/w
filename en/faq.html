Event Planning FAQ

Useful answers before we start planning together.

What events does Weiß Events plan?

Corporate events, bespoke events, incentives, private celebrations, galas, sports events, team building and individual special formats.

Do you offer fixed packages?

No. Every event is developed from a personal brief and can be planned as full service or with selected services.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Concepts and productions can be planned in Germany, Europe and selected destinations worldwide.

How does a project start?

With a non-binding conversation about the occasion, objectives, guests, location, timing and budget framework.
