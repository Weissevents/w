How we plan events

We begin with your objectives, audience, location preferences and budget framework. We then develop a concept and coordinate suitable venues, suppliers, logistics, design and the event schedule.

Event agency in Germany and internationally

Based in Dinslaken, Germany, Weiß Events supports events in Germany and international destinations. The scope of planning and on-site coordination is agreed individually for each project.

Which event service is right for you?

Corporate events bring teams, customers or brands together. Incentive travel rewards and motivates. Destination events use a location as an integral part of the experience. For an event that does not fit a category, explore our bespoke event services.
